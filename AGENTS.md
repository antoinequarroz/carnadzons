## Development servers

- Always use Portly (`portly ...`) to start, stop, restart, inspect, or keep local development servers running.
- Start with `portly status`. Reuse a healthy managed server when in scope.
- Use `portly temp '<command>' --path <folder> --timeout 30m` for bounded previews, then stop the owned temporary job after verification.
- Never launch persistent development servers directly, in the background, or through another supervisor.

## Existing design

- Preserve the homepage temple geometry, Cleopatra/Onest typography, palette, scene markers and reversible scroll transitions unless the user asks to change them.
- Secondary pages are generated by `node scripts/build-pages.mjs`. Edit `scripts/site-pages.json` for content. Keep the homepage and entree-egypte alias synchronized.
- Do not present past edition programmes, prices, transport or child carnival dates as confirmed for 2027.
